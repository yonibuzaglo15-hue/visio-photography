# VISIO Photography

VISIO is a Hebrew, RTL photography studio (owner יונתן בוזגלו). The public site already sells property photography: portfolio, packages, booking, charter, about. This pass adds scroll as a timeline. It does not rewrite the copy, prices, or routes.

## 1. Vibe
Editorial, photographic, quiet luxury. A printed lookbook that moves when you scroll. References: a darkroom contact sheet, a viewfinder closing on a frame, a gallery you walk.

## 2. Scroll journey
Masthead, then the cover photograph, then recent jobs as a sideways strip, then the published numbers, partners, and the booking ask. Then the selected-work poster, the catalog, packages, the booking form, the charter, the about text, one held quotation over a photograph, and the footer.

## 3. Energy
Calm at the masthead. The cover holds. The strip of jobs is the quicker passage. Numbers are quiet. The selected-work poster is the second hold. Packages, the form, and the charter are for reading. The quotation is the last hold, then the footer resolves.

## 4. Feeling curve
- Cover: confidence. The photograph is already framed, and the line arrives whole.
- Recent work: curiosity. Real jobs travel sideways under the hand.
- Numbers: steadiness. The figures already printed on the site count once and stay.
- Selected work: recognition. One interior fills the frame and the title assembles.
- Catalog, packages, booking, charter: usefulness. The working parts of the site, revealed as they arrive, not pinned into emptiness.
- Quotation: agreement. The existing line sits on a photograph and holds.
- Footer: arrival. The same ask, plus the WhatsApp number that was specified as a contact to keep.

## 5. Peak
The cover. A visitor would say: "It's the site where the photograph tightens in a viewfinder as you scroll, and the line נדל״ן. בצילום שמוכר. locks into place." It lives in the cover act, which has the longest pin (3.6 viewport-heights).

## 6. Signature seed
The frame should behave like a camera, not like a template fade.

## 7. Distance from premium-minimal
Stay on the existing editorial monochrome (Bodoni, Frank Ruhl Libre, Heebo). Do not restyle the studio into another palette.

## 8. One world or distinct scenes
Distinct scenes. There is no footage of a continuous flight, and inventing one over real properties would misrepresent the work.

## Assets
Existing property photographs only. No generated stills, no generated video.

## Tell-someone
It's the site where scrolling frames the photograph the way a camera does.

## Authored silence
The booking form, the charter, and the package grid are read, not pinned. A still form is not dead scroll. Reduced motion drops travel and keeps the type at full strength.
